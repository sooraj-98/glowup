Questions people ask

Do I need a consultation before a peel?
Yes. If it's your first peel with us, book the free consultation first so we can check your skin is ready.
Is a facial OK for sensitive skin?
Yes. Tell us about your skin when you book and we'll use our fragrance-free products.
How do I cancel or move a booking?
Reply to your confirmation email or call us at least 24 hours before your visit. There's no fee.
Do you sell gift cards?
Yes, in any amount from $25. Ask at the front desk or in the chat.
Is there parking?
Free parking is behind the studio. Look for the peach door.
How do I pay?
We take all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payment is after your treatment.
